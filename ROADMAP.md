# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to fostering an open, respectful, and constructive environment for all contributors.

Participation in this project requires adherence to this Code of Conduct.

---

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and professional.
- Provide constructive feedback.
- Assume good intent.
- Focus on technical merit.
- Help maintain a welcoming environment.

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or hostility.
- Personal attacks or inflammatory language.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Disruptive or malicious behavior.

---

## Enforcement

Project maintainers reserve the right to remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

Severe violations may result in temporary or permanent bans from participation.

---

## Reporting

Security or conduct concerns may be reported through the process defined in `SECURITY.md`.
